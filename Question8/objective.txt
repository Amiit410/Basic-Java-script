Objective:
Create a User Registration Form with strong validation.
Fields:
 Username
 Email
 Password
 Confirm Password
Validation Rules:
 Username → minimum 5 characters
 Email → valid email format
 Password →
o Minimum 8 characters
o At least 1 uppercase
o 1 lowercase
o 1 digit
o 1 special character
 Confirm password must match password.
